## 4. Hardware safety; no warranty

Cutting machines have sharp blades and moving parts and are controlled by the
commands this software sends. **Cutting support has not yet been tested on real
hardware.** Incorrect commands could cut in unexpected places, cut through the
mat, damage the blade, the machine, or the material, or cause injury.

- Test with the virtual cutter and the tool-path preview first.
- Start with scrap material, low force, and low speed.
- Stay with the machine while it runs, and keep hands, hair, and loose
  clothing away from the carriage.
- Know how to stop the machine (the cancel button, or its power switch).
